Your Questions Answered

What If We Don't Like the Design?

You get 2 full revision rounds during the design phase. We don't move to development until you approve. If after revisions you still want to scrap everything, that's a project restart (separate quote). But in 8+ years, we've never had to do this.

What If the Timeline Slips?

We hit deadlines 95% of the time. The #1 reason for delays is late content delivery from clients. If WE cause the delay, you don't pay until delivery. If delays are on your end, we'll adjust timeline accordingly.

What If We're Not Happy at Launch?

You have a full 2-week post-launch support period. Any bugs or issues present at launch are fixed immediately, unlimited. We also provide a detailed acceptance checklist before final payment.

What If Technology Changes?

Next.js is the industry standard and backed by Vercel (valued at $2.5B). It's not going anywhere. We build to web standards, ensuring future compatibility. Plus, you own all source code.
