# 1. Project Overview

## What We're Building

A premium, mobile-optimized website that turns visitors into members.

### The Challenge
Your current situation (or if you're starting fresh): You need a digital presence that matches the intensity and authenticity of Arena Boxing. Not a corporate gym website. Not an Instagram-friendly facade. A real platform that converts serious athletes and first-timers alike.

### The Solution
An 8-page website built specifically for combat sports gyms:
- **Cinematic landing experience** that stops people in their tracks
- **Booking integration** that makes it stupid-easy to reserve classes
- **Mobile-first design** because 70% of traffic comes from phones

**Not set-it-and-forget-it.** We build it to evolve with your business.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can we change the scope mid-project?
Yes, but it requires a change order. We'll provide a quote for additional work with timeline impact. Small tweaks within revisions are fine.

### What if we're late on content?
The launch date pushes. We'll proceed with placeholder content and integrate yours when ready. Better to get it right than rush it.

### Can we launch without all pages?
Technically yes, but not recommended. We can prioritize critical pages (Home, Timetable, Membership, Booking) and add others post-launch.

### What if we don't like the designs?
Two full revision rounds are included. We'll work with you until you're happy. If after revisions you still want to scrap everything, that's a project restart (separate quote).

### Do you write the copy for us?
We'll edit and polish what you provide, but you know your business best. We provide a content guide with examples and character limits. For professional copywriting, we can refer you to a partner ($1,500 add-on).

### What if Hapana changes their API and breaks the booking widget?
During the project: We'll handle it.
After 2-week support period: Hourly billing ($200/hour) or covered under Care Plan.

### Can we host it ourselves?
Technically yes, but we strongly recommend Vercel for Next.js sites. DIY hosting often causes performance and deployment issues. If you insist, we'll provide deployment instructions but can't guarantee uptime/performance.

### Can we switch Care Plans later?
Absolutely. Upgrade anytime (immediate), downgrade at next billing cycle. Cancel anytime after 3-month minimum term with 30 days notice.
