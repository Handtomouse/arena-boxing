## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I pause my plan if I go on vacation?
**Essential/Professional**: No pause option. Plan continues, unused hours expire.
**Premium**: Yes, you may pause for up to 1 month per year. 30 days notice required.

### What happens if I need more hours than my plan includes?
We'll notify you when you're approaching your limit. You can:
1. Approve overflow billing at $150/hour
2. Defer non-urgent work to next month
3. Upgrade to a higher plan (takes effect immediately)

### Can I use banked hours for a big project?
Yes! Banked hours (Professional/Premium only) can be used for larger projects. Example: If you've banked 8 hours and have 12 current hours (Professional plan), you can tackle a 20-hour project in one month.

### What if the Hapana API changes and breaks the booking widget?
**Essential**: We'll notify you and provide a quote for the fix (billed hourly).
**Professional/Premium**: Covered under plan. We'll coordinate with Hapana and implement necessary updates.

### Do you offer custom plans for multi-location gyms?
Yes! Contact us for enterprise pricing. Typical discounts:
- 2-3 locations: 15% off per additional site
- 4+ locations: 25% off per additional site
- Shared resources (trainers, classes) across sites: Additional discounts available

### Can I switch plans mid-month?
**Upgrade**: Yes, immediate with pro-rated credit.
**Downgrade**: Takes effect at next billing cycle to prevent hour banking abuse.

### What's your response time if the site goes down?
- **Essential**: 8 hours (critical issues only)
- **Professional**: 4 hours
- **Premium**: 2 hours + 24/7 emergency phone line

### Is hosting included in all plans?
Yes! Vercel Pro hosting (~$20/month value) is included in all care plan tiers. We manage deployment, SSL, CDN, and uptime.

### What if I want to cancel?
After your minimum 3-month term, provide 30 days written notice. No penalties. You'll receive a pro-rated refund for any unused days in the current month.

### Can I get a discount for annual prepayment?
Yes! Pay annually and save:
- **Essential**: $3,228/year (save $360 = 10%)
- **Professional**: $6,468/year (save $720 = 10%)
- **Premium**: $10,788/year (save $1,200 = 10%)

### Do you offer à la carte support without a monthly plan?
Yes, but at a higher rate:
- **Hourly Rate (no plan)**: $200/hour (vs. $150/hour with plan)
- **Minimum Billable**: 4 hours
- **Response Time**: 48 business hours (no SLA)
- **No Proactive Monitoring**: Reactive support only
