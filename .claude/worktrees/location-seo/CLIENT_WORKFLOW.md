# Client Workflow — Arena Boxing Website Project

## Overview
This document outlines the complete workflow for delivering the Arena Boxing website project, from initial discovery through to launch and handover.

---

## Phase 1: Discovery & Strategy (Week 1)

### Deliverables
- **Kickoff Meeting** (90 minutes)
  - Project goals and success metrics
  - Target audience analysis
  - Competitive landscape review
  - Technical requirements gathering

- **Discovery Document**
  - Brand positioning summary
  - User personas (3 primary archetypes)
  - Site map and navigation structure
  - Content inventory and gaps
  - Third-party integrations (Hapana, Instagram, Google Maps)

### Client Responsibilities
- Provide brand assets (logo, fonts, color palette, imagery)
- Share existing analytics/user data if available
- Confirm Hapana booking system credentials
- Identify key stakeholders and decision-makers

### Sign-off Required
✅ Discovery document approved by client before proceeding to design

**Timeline**: 5 business days

---

## Phase 2: Design Approval (Weeks 2-3)

### Round 1: Wireframes & Style Direction
**Deliverables:**
- Low-fidelity wireframes for 8 pages (landing, home, timetable, membership, booking, about, location, FAQ)
- Design system preview (typography, colors, components)
- Moodboard showcasing gothic aesthetic + premium feel

**Feedback Cycle:**
- Client reviews wireframes (3 business days)
- Revision meeting (60 minutes)
- Updated wireframes delivered (2 business days)

**Sign-off Required:**
✅ Wireframes and design direction approved

---

### Round 2: High-Fidelity Designs
**Deliverables:**
- Desktop designs for all 8 pages
- Mobile designs for all 8 pages
- Interactive prototype (Figma/similar)
- Component library showcase
- Animation specifications

**Feedback Cycle:**
- Client reviews designs (5 business days)
- Design review meeting (90 minutes)
- Minor revisions (2 rounds included)
- Final design sign-off

**Sign-off Required:**
✅ All page designs approved for development

**Timeline**: 10 business days total

---

## Phase 4: Feedback Cycles

### Structured Feedback Process
**Tool**: Shared feedback document (Google Docs/Notion)

**Categories:**
- 🔴 **Critical**: Breaks functionality, blocks launch
- 🟡 **Important**: Significant UX issue, needs fixing pre-launch
- 🟢 **Nice-to-have**: Enhancement for post-launch consideration

**Response Times:**
- Critical issues: 24-48 hours
- Important issues: 3-5 business days
- Nice-to-have: Logged for post-launch roadmap

**Included Revisions:**
- 2 rounds of design revisions (Phase 2)
- 2 rounds of dev revisions per milestone (Phase 3)
- 1 final QA round before launch (Phase 5)

**Additional Revisions:**
Billed at hourly rate ($150/hour) with 2-hour minimum

### Content Required from Client
**Due Date**: End of Week 6

**Required Content:**
- [ ] Final copy for all pages (home, timetable, membership, booking, about, location, FAQ)
- [ ] Trainer bios and headshots (3 trainers minimum)
- [ ] Class descriptions and schedule (actual timetable data)
- [ ] Membership pricing (final confirmed pricing)
- [ ] High-resolution images (hero backgrounds, facility photos)
- [ ] Contact information (phone, email, address, hours)
- [ ] Social media links (Instagram, Facebook, etc.)

**Format:**
- Copy: Google Doc with clear page labels
- Images: Dropbox/Drive folder, minimum 1920x1080 for hero images

- Image specification guide

- Development proceeds with placeholder content
- Final content integrated during Week 7

**Timeline**: 3-5 business days for content integration

- [ ] Tested on Android (Chrome)

- [ ] Tested on desktop (Chrome, Safari, Firefox)

**Content:**
- [ ] No Lorem Ipsum placeholder text
- [ ] All images have alt text
- [ ] SEO metadata complete (titles, descriptions, OG images)
- [ ] Contact information accurate

**Accessibility:**
- [ ] Keyboard navigation functional
- [ ] Screen reader tested

**Technical:**
- Domain DNS configuration (client provides domain access)
- SSL certificate installation
- Production deployment to Vercel/hosting platform
- Analytics setup (Google Analytics/similar)

**Administrative:**
- Final invoice issued (see Payment Schedule below)

- Handover documentation prepared

### Launch Day
- Site goes live (coordinated time agreed in advance)
- Smoke test performed immediately post-launch

**Timeline**: 5 business days

## Change Request Process

### Scope Changes
Any request outside the agreed deliverables follows this process:

1. **Client submits change request** via email/project management tool
2. **Impact assessment provided within 48 hours:**
   - Time impact (additional days)
   - Cost impact (additional fees)
   - Timeline impact (new launch date)
3. **Client approves or declines** in writing
4. **Change order issued** with updated SOW and payment terms
5. **Work proceeds** upon signed change order + payment

### Minor vs. Major Changes
**Minor (No additional cost):**
- Copy tweaks (if within revision limits)
- Color adjustments (if within design system)
- Small layout refinements (if within approved design)

**Major (Additional cost):**
- New page additions
- New features/functionality
- Complete design overhauls
- Third-party integrations not in original scope

## Risk Management

### Potential Delays & Mitigation
| Risk | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|---------------------|
| Late content delivery | Proceed with placeholders; final integration scheduled separately |
| Design approval delays | Grace period of 3 business days, then escalation to stakeholder |
| Third-party API issues (Hapana) | Fallback to manual booking form; integrate API when stable |
| Client availability gaps | Async approval process via email; recorded video walkthroughs |
| Scope creep | Strict change request process; all additions require written approval |

### Launch Blockers
Work will not proceed to launch if:
- ❌ Final payment not received
- ❌ Critical QA issues unresolved
- ❌ Client has not signed off on UAT
- ❌ Required content not provided
