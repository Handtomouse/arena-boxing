## 3. Timetable Page (/timetable)

Your class schedule - simple but crucial.

### Text Needed ✏️

#### Page Header
- [ ] **Page Title**
  - Character limit: 2-5 words
  - Example: "Class Timetable" / "Weekly Schedule" / "Training Times"

- [ ] **Intro Text** (optional)
  - Character limit: 50-100 words
  - Example: "Book online 24/7. All classes are 60 minutes. First class free for new members."

#### Class Schedule Data
For EACH class type, provide:
- [ ] **Class Name** (e.g., "Boxing Fundamentals", "Fight Fitness", "Sparring Sessions")
- [ ] **Day(s) of week** (Monday, Tuesday, etc.)
- [ ] **Time** (e.g., 6:00 AM, 6:30 PM)
- [ ] **Duration** (usually 60 minutes)
- [ ] **Instructor name** (e.g., "with Sarah" or "with Tom")
- [ ] **Difficulty level** (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced / All Levels)
- [ ] **Spots available** (e.g., "12 spots" or "Unlimited")

**Easiest format:** Send us a spreadsheet (Excel or Google Sheets) with these columns. We'll handle the rest.

#### Class Type Descriptions (for reference section)
For each class type (usually 4-6 types):
- [ ] **Class Name**
- [ ] **Short Description** (30-50 words)
- [ ] **Who it's for** (e.g., "Perfect for beginners" or "Advanced fighters only")

Example:
- **Boxing Fundamentals**: Learn proper stance, footwork, and basic combinations. No experience needed. 60 minutes of technique work and bag drills. Perfect for first-timers.

## Image Guidelines Summary

Let's keep it simple. Here's what makes a good photo for your website:

### DO:
✅ Use high-resolution photos (bigger is better - we can resize)
✅ Show real people training (get their permission!)
✅ Capture the vibe - dark, gritty, authentic
✅ Include action shots - movement makes photos come alive
✅ Mix wide shots and close-ups
✅ Natural lighting or dramatic gym lighting
✅ Show your actual gym, equipment, trainers

### DON'T:
❌ Use blurry or low-quality photos
❌ Send overly edited/filtered Instagram photos
❌ Use stock photos (we want YOUR gym)
❌ Include people's faces without permission
❌ Send photos with visible competitor logos
❌ Over-pose or stage photos (keep it real!)

### Technical Stuff (Don't Stress!)
- **File format:** JPG or PNG (we'll optimize them)
- **Size:** At least 1920x1080 for hero images, 800x600 for others
- **File names:** Anything is fine, we'll rename them
- **How many:** See each page section - roughly 25-35 images total

**Don't have professional photos?** That's okay! Use your phone:
- Clean the lens
- Good lighting (daylight is best)
- Multiple shots of the same thing (we'll pick the best)
- Focus on what makes your gym special
